Three distinct logo ideas for House of Zion, shown in the cinematic website setting. These are concept studies for discussion, not finished vector masters.

01Recommended direction

Sculpted wordmark

The name does the work. Elegant, expressive lettering with an editorial feel.

ProsWarm elegance. Reads as a complete name. Sits naturally over cinematic interiors.

ConsThe finest details may soften at small sizes. Needs precise letterform refinement.

02The compact signature

Joined HZ

An interlocking signature, paired with the full name for recognition.

ProsA compact shape for small applications. Gives the identity a recognisable symbol.

ConsH/Z readability needs refinement. The initials should never obscure the name.

03The spatial expression

Open Space

An open geometric form: a simple suggestion of space and possibility.

ProsThe clearest architectural cue. A structured symbol that pairs with modern interiors.

ConsCould feel like a property brand. Needs care to preserve warmth and personality.

Start with 01.

Its advantage is an elegant, readable name with enough expression to sit over the cinematic interior. The trade-off is small-scale detail. Refine the lettering and test a simpler small version before committing.
